# Small Arms Survey

For 25 years, the Small Arms Survey has provided impartial, evidence-based, and policy-relevant knowledge on all aspects of small arms and armed violence to governments, policymakers, researchers, and civil society.

[More about us](https://www.smallarmssurvey.org/who%5Fwe%5Fare/vision%5Fmission)
