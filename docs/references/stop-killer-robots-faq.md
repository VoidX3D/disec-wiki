## Stop Killer Robots

Since 2013, our global campaign calls for new international law on autonomy in weapons systems. With 300+ member organisations in over 70 countries, we provide a united voice with national, regional and international affect. Stop Killer Robots is a vibrant, inclusive team driving change to protect our shared humanity for the future.
