                Permanent Forum on People of African Descent
                Note by the Secretariat

                1.    The General Assembly, in paragraph 4 of its resolution 75/314 on the
                establishment of the Permanent Forum on People of African Descent, adopted on
                2 August 2021, decided that the Permanent Forum would sub mit an annual report to
                the Human Rights Council and the Assembly on its activities, including advice and
                recommendations for future thematic subjects for their consideration, and engage in
                the interactive dialogue.
                2.  Pursuant to that request, the report of the Permanent Forum is contained in
                document A/HRC/54/68. The Secretariat therefore has the honour to refer the General
                Assembly to that report.
