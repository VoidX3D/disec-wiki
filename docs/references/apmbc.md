## Siem Reap-Angkor Review Conference

**The Fifth Review Conference of the States Parties to the Convention took place Siem Reap, Cambodia from 25-29 November 2024\. It was in Cambodia, where 13 years prior, the international community met at the Eleventh Meeting of the States Parties (11MSP). Cambodia is also considered one of the birthplaces of the mine ban movement.**

For more information please visit,

[**Siem Reap-Angkor Summit**](https://www.apminebanconvention.org/en/review-conferences/5revcon)
