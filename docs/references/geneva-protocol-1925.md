1925 Geneva Protocol
Protocol for the Prohibition of the Use in War of Asphyxiating, Poisonous or
Other Gases, and of Bacteriological Methods of Warfare

The 1925 Geneva Protocol prohibits the use of chemical and biological weapons in war. The
Protocol was drawn up and signed at a conference which was held in Geneva under the
auspices of the League of Nations from 4 May to 17 June 1925, and it entered into force on 8
February 1928.
