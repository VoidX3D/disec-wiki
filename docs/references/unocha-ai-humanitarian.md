GHO

Mid-Year Review of the Global Humanitarian Overview: Delivering for People in Crisis against the Odds

 The 2026 Mid-Year review of the GHO : Delivering for People in Crisis against the Odds analyses global crises around the world, highlighting that violence, climate shock and health emergencies are the main drivers of needs. In June 2026, humanitarian partners are appealing for US$34 billion to assist 252 million people in crisis, with the immediate priority being to save 89 million lives. The mid-year review presents a picture of humanitarian’s achievements during the first five months of 2026 as well as what happens when aid comes under attack.

[Read more](https://humanitarianaction.info/document/mid-year-review-global-humanitarian-overview-delivering-people-crisis-against-odds)

Operations

Escalation in the Middle East and Beyond: The Humanitarian Response

 Issued in March 2026, Escalation in the Middle East and Beyond: The Humanitarian Response outlines the rapidly worsening humanitarian consequences of expanding hostilities across the Middle East and neighbouring regions. Civilians are bearing the brunt of the violence through deaths, displacement, destruction of essential services, and severe constraints on humanitarian access. The escalation has triggered the most significant global humanitarian supply‑chain disruptions since COVID‑19, driving up costs and delaying life‑saving assistance far beyond the region. Humanitarian partners are responding across Iran, Lebanon, the Occupied Palestinian Territory, Syria, Afghanistan and beyond, prioritizing life‑saving aid, protection of civilians, support to local responders, and respect for international humanitarian law, while urgently calling for de‑escalation and flexible funding to sustain the response.

[Read more](https://humanitarianaction.info/document/escalation-middle-east-and-beyond-humanitarian-response)

GHO

Global Humanitarian Overview 2026

 The GHO 2026 presents an analysis of global crises and needs and the humanitarian plans to address them. In 2026, humanitarian partners are appealing for almost US$33 billion to assist 135 million people in crisis, with the immediate priority being to mobilize $23 billion to save 87 million lives. The GHO presents a collective picture of humanitarians' achievements and, conversely, what happens when humanitarians are unable to deliver aid.

[Explore the report](https://humanitarianaction.info/document/global-humanitarian-overview-2026)
