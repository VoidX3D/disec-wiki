# Treaties: Membership & Status

Consolidated membership, signature and ratification status for every instrument in
the committee's reference library. Status reflects official UN Treaty Collection,
IAEA, OPCW and ICBL data (2024–2025). "Party" = bound by ratification/accession.

### Notes

- **Iran** is party to the NPT (1970), CCW (1997) and CWC (1997) but is **not party**
  to the Mine Ban, Cluster Munitions, TPNW or ATT (only signed), and has not
  ratified the CTBT.
- **Iran has not accepted CCW Amended Protocol II** (landmines) — consistent with
  its rejection of the Ottawa Treaty. It is also not party to **Protocol V (ERW)**,
  relevant given its Iran–Iraq War contamination.
- **United States** is not party to the Mine Ban, Cluster Munitions or ATT (signed
  2013, never ratified), and has signed but not ratified the CTBT.
- **Russia, China and India** are non-parties to the Mine Ban and Cluster
  Munitions Conventions.

## Non-proliferation treaty universe — "states after WWII" context

States that have **possessed nuclear weapons since 1945**: the **US** (1945),
**USSR/Russia** (1949), **UK** (1952), **France** (1960), **China** (1964),
**India** (1974), **Pakistan** (1998), **North Korea** (2006), **Israel**
(undeclared, c. 1960s). Iran **has not** — it is an NPT non-nuclear-weapon state.
