## Internet users by age group

Estimated distribution of the ~73M connected users by age bracket (DataReportal
Digital 2025, "users" = individuals 16+ using the internet in the last 12 months).

| Age group | Share of internet users | Approx. users |
|---|---|---|
| 16–24 | ~15% | ~11 million |
| 25–34 | ~22% | ~16 million |
| 35–44 | ~21% | ~15 million |
| 45–54 | ~16% | ~12 million |
| 55–64 | ~12% | ~9 million |
| 65+ | ~7% | ~5 million |
| 16–24 not using internet | — | significant |

**Digital divide by age:** penetration is near-universal among 16–34s (upwards of
95%) and falls sharply after 55. Rural connectivity and affordability lag urban
centres — the same communities whose infrastructure any future remotely-operated or
autonomous military systems would rely upon.

## Why this matters for the committee

- **Platforms are the battleground for narrative:** the same networks used to organize
  civil society are the ones states propose to surveil or shut down "in the interest of
  national security" — a direct tension with LAWS transparency debates.
- **AI in Persian:** Farsi is a relatively under-resourced language for commercial
  AI (low LLM training weight), but Iran has invested in domestic models (e.g. the
  Aria Foundation Model, released 2024). Language coverage affects AI accuracy,
  and therefore affects who can be safely targeted by AI-assisted systems.
- **Connectivity = sensor data:** 90% mobile-first internet means massive
  location/app data is generated domestically — relevant to debates on
  AI-enabled targeting and surveillance, and to the "dual-use" nature of data.
