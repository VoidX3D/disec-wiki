# Poverty, Income & Living Standards

> **How to read this section.** Iranian poverty statistics are contested. The
> Statistical Centre of Iran (SCI) computes a **national poverty line** from household
> expenditure surveys; the World Bank computes **international PPP poverty lines**
> ($2.15, $3.65, $6.85 per day, 2017 prices). The two methodologies disagree sharply,
> and both were distorted by the 2018 sanctions shock, COVID-19 and 40%+ inflation.
> Exact published values are given below with their source year — margin should be
> assumed wherever a range is shown.

## Poverty headcount — World Bank (2017 PPP international lines)

| Line | 2018 (pre-sanctions) | 2020 (COVID era) | Latest (2021–22) |
|---|---|---|---|
| $2.15/day (extreme) | 0.4% | 1.7% | ~2.0% (est.) |
| $3.65/day (lower-middle) | 10.3% | 15.4% | ~18% (est.) |
| $6.85/day (upper-middle) | 36.3% | 48.2% | ~55% (est.) |

> The **$6.85/day** line is the relevant benchmark for Iran (an upper-middle-income
> economy): on World Bank estimates roughly **half the population fell below it**
> after the sanctions/COVID/inflation shock. Absolute numbers of people under the
> $6.85 line: ~**40–46 million** in 2020–22.

| Year | National poverty rate | Urban | Rural | Source |

| 2017 (1396) | ~17.5% | ~13.5% | ~27.5% | SCI |
| 2018 (1397) | ~20.5% | ~16.5% | ~30.5% | SCI |
| 2019 (1398) | ~25.0% | ~20.5% | ~36.0% | SCI |
| 2020 (1399) | ~28.5% | ~24.0% | ~40.0% | SCI/World Bank |
| 2021 (1400) | ~29.5% | ~25.0% | ~41.0% | SCI/World Bank |
| 2022 (1401) | **~30.1%** | ~26.0% | ~41.5% | World Bank/SCI |

> Iran's own statistics place **~30% of the population (≈ 26–27 million people)**
> below the national absolute poverty line in 2022 — up from ~17.5% in 2017. The
> government has disputed some of these estimates, arguing the national basket
> over-weights urban consumption.

## Poverty: key structural facts

- **Household expenditure vs income:** surveys show expenditure (paid partly in
  subsidized goods) exceeds income, implying hidden coping (savings drawdown, debt).
- **Poverty depth:** the "poverty gap" roughly doubled 2018→2022; the poor are
  deeper below the line than pre-sanctions.
- **Energy subsidy reform:** 2022–2024 subsidy cuts (fuel, bread, electricity)
  pushed the bottom decile hardest — the poorest decile spends a larger share of
  income on energy than the richest decile.
- **Regional concentration:** Sistan–Baluchestan (southeast), Kurdistan,
  Khuzestan and the eastern border provinces have poverty rates 2–3× the national
  average (Sistan–Baluchestan estimated >50% in some years).
- **Child poverty:** UNICEF estimated **~27% of children live in poverty** (2021).

## Income & wages

| Indicator | Value | Year | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Median household income (urban, monthly) | ~24.1M rials | 2022 | SCI household survey |
| Median household income (rural, monthly) | ~13.8M rials | 2022 | SCI |
| Average monthly salary (private sector) | ~22–25M rials | 2023 | CBI / Maskan |
| Statutory monthly minimum wage | 71.65M rials* | 2023 (FY1402) | Supreme Labour Council |
| Statutory monthly minimum wage | 85.66M rials* | 2024 (FY1403) | Supreme Labour Council |
| Minimum wage vs median household income | ~2.4–3.1× | 2024 | ratio |
| Share of population with any pension | ~20% | 2022 | World Bank |

\* Statutory minimum wage includes all subsidies/allowances; base minimum wage was
~52.9M rials/month in 1402 and ~71.6M rials/month in 1403. At the ~285,000
rial/USD free-market rate these are roughly **US$150–300/month** — well below the
official-rate equivalent, reflecting rial depreciation.

> **Real wage trajectory:** after inflation, the real minimum wage has fallen by
> roughly **40–50% since 2018**. Purchasing power of the median salary in 2024 was
> near 2010 levels in real terms (CBI / SCI inflation series).

## Minimum wage vs poverty line (comparison)

Using the SCI national poverty line for a single-person household (~13–16M
rials/month in 2022–23):

| Item | Monthly value (rials, 2023) | Ratio to poverty line |
|---|---|---|
| Statutory minimum wage | 52.9M (base) | ~3.3× single person |
| Statutory minimum wage (incl. allowances) | 71.6M | ~4.5× single person |
| Median household income (urban) | ~24.1M | ~1.5× single person |

> **Why it looks contradictory:** a single minimum-wage earner is above the official
> single-person poverty line, yet ~30% of the population is "poor" — because the
> line is household-based (average household = ~3.2 people), most households have
> only one income earner, and female participation is ~14%, so household *per-capita*
> income is far below the single-earner figure.
