## Sanctions exposure — financial

- **US** primary + secondary sanctions; **EU** restrictive measures; **UN** measures
  lifted under JCPOA (2016), snapback provisions lapsed Oct 2025.
- **SWIFT** access for Iranian banks cut in 2018; reinstated under JCPOA, cut
  again after 2018 US withdrawal.
- **OFAC** SDN list includes IRGC, its financial arm (Khatam al-Anbiya), banks
  (Bank Mellat, Bank Saderat, Post Bank), and hundreds of entities/persons.
- **Iranian settlement channels:** barter (oil-for-goods), crypto (USDT-heavy
  grey market), China/Russia payment corridors (CIPS, SPFS), and the
  **INSTEX** (Europe, largely dormant).
