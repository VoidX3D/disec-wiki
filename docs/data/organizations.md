# Organizations: Membership & Roles

Every organization Iran belongs to (or engages with) and what Iran does there —
the institutional map of Iran's foreign policy.

## United Nations system

| Body | Iran status | Role |
|---|---|---|
| UN General Assembly | Full member | Active in disarmament (DISEC) |
| UN Security Council | Non-permanent 2025–2026 | Sits during LAWS negotiations |
| IAEA | Member (1958) | Nuclear safeguards — core dossier |
| OPCW | Member (1997) | CWC implementation |
| WHO | Member | Health cooperation |
| UNESCO | Member | Heritage, education |
| UNODC | Member | Drugs (Afghan border) |
| UNDP | Member | Development programs |
| Human Rights Council | Non-member since 2020 (held 2018–20) | Criticized record |

## Disarmament & arms control venues

| Forum | Iran's position |
|---|---|
| **CCW GGE on LAWS** | Active participant; supports binding instrument |
| **UNGA First Committee (DISEC)** | Voting bloc leader on LAWS |
| **NPT Review Conferences** | NNWS bloc; criticizes NWS non-compliance |
| **Conference on Disarmament (CD)** | Member; backs Fissile Material Cut-off negotiation |
| **UN Register of Conventional Arms** | Reports partially |

## Regional & defence organizations Iran is NOT in

| Organization | Status | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **NATO** | Non-member (hostile) | NATO seen as US-led threat |
| **CSTO** | Observer only | Iran closer to Russia but not bound |
| **GCC** | Non-member (tension with Saudi/UAE) | Rival Gulf bloc |
| **Five Eyes** | Non-member | Western intelligence |
| **Quad / AUKUS** | Non-member | Indo-Pacific anti-China |

## The organizations that matter for LAWS specifically

| Organization | Iran's angle |
|---|---|
| **UN CCW** | Primary negotiating forum for LAWS protocol |
| **GGE on LAWS** | Where Iran advances "sovereign right to defensive autonomy" |
| **UNIDIR** | Think-tank; publishes on AI/autonomy Iran cites |
| **ICRC** | Humanitarian law counterpart |
| **Stop Killer Robots** | Civil-society coalition Iran engages rhetorically |
| **IISS / SIPRI** | Data Iran cites for asymmetric balance |
| **EU (in CCW)** | Opposing ban-first bloc |
| **NATO** | The actor Iran's LAWS stance is shaped against |
