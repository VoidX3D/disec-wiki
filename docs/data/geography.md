## Topography & terrain

- **Zagros Mountains** — western spine, ~1,500 km; where most of the Iran–Iraq
  war frontline and today's Kurdish/security zones sit.
- **Alborz Mountains** — north, includes **Mount Damavand** (5,610 m, highest peak,
  a stratovolcano ~70 km NE of Tehran).
- **Dasht-e Kavir** and **Dasht-e Lut** — the two great central deserts; Lut is a
  UNESCO site and among the hottest places on Earth (surface temps >70°C).
- **Caspian lowlands** — fertile, humid strip north of the Alborz.
- **Persian Gulf coast** — narrow arid coastal plain; the energy heartland
  (Khuzestan, Bushehr, Hormozgan).
- **~85% of terrain** is arid/semi-arid; only ~9% arable.

## Climate

| Region | Climate | Temp range |
|---|---|---|
| Caspian coast | Humid subtropical | Mild winters, ~10–35°C |
| Tehran / plateau | Semi-arid, continental | −5 to 40°C |
| Persian Gulf coast | Hot desert | 25–50°C, high humidity |
| Central deserts | Hyper-arid | 0 to 50°C+ |
| Zagros | Mediterranean/mountain | Cold winters, snow |

**Water crisis:** drought-driven; precipitation ~25% below long-term average in the
2020s; Lake Urmia shrunk ~90% from its 1990s peak.
