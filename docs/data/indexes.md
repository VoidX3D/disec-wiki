## Quick-interpretation for delegates

- **Iran scores "High" on human development but "Authoritarian/Not free" on
  governance** — the single most useful dual fact in committee.
- **CPI 25/100, Press Freedom ~178/180, Democracy Index ~154/167** position Iran
  in the bottom decile of most governance indexes.
- **Military power is rated ~17th globally** but economic and technology indexes
  are mid-pack — supporting Iran's "asymmetric strength despite sanctions" story.
- **MPI 0.003 + HDI 0.799** is the evidence for "low multidimensional poverty but
  contested income poverty" — delegates can use MPI vs national-line poverty
  (30%) discrepancy as a methodological talking point.
