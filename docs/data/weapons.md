## Chemical & biological

- **Chemical weapons:** none (CWC party since 1997; declared past program ended
  1988; Iran was a CW victim 1983–88).
- **Biological weapons:** no known program (BWC party 1973).

## Nuclear

- **Status:** NPT non-nuclear-weapon state.
- **Enrichment:** 60% U-235 stockpile (2024–25, IAEA-verified), enrichment
  infrastructure at Natanz, Fordow, Isfahan.
- **No nuclear weapons**; no weaponization reported by IAEA.
