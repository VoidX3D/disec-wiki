Since the 2020 HDR, Iran has hovered just below the 0.800 "Very High" threshold.
The **gender development index** (GDI) gap is significant: female HDI is well below
male HDI, driven by labor-force participation rather than schooling.

> **Sanctions context:** the economy is dominated by hydrocarbons and heavily
> sanctioned. Effective (shadow) unemployment is far higher than the official figure —
> particularly among the young — which directly feeds the demographic and
> technology arguments around remote/autonomous warfare.

Iran's **female labor-force participation (~14%) is among the lowest in the world**
despite high female educational attainment — a core inequality data point delegates
can use when discussing who builds, funds and fights with military AI.

## Inequality & poverty

- Absolute poverty headcount ratio (national poverty line) ~20% (2022–23 est.).
- The **Gini coefficient (~0.40)** has risen since the mid-2010s; subsidy removal and
  inflation have widened the gap.
- The **Top 1%** income share has grown steadily under sanctions-era redistribution.
