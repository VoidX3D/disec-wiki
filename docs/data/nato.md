## What NATO is

The **North Atlantic Treaty Organization** — a mutual-defense alliance under the
1949 Washington Treaty (Article 5: an attack on one is an attack on all).
Iran regards NATO as the military wing of the US-led order and a core threat,
especially NATO's eastward expansion and its missile-defense architecture.

## Military spending — 2% GDP commitment

NATO's 2014 Wales commitment: **2% of GDP on defence** (2023 Hague summit raised
expectations to 2%+ with a "reinvestment" target). 2025 estimates (NATO + SIPRI):

| Tier | States | Spending |
|---|---|---|
| >3% of GDP | Poland, Greece, Estonia, US | Top spenders |
| 2–3% | ~15 members (UK, Germany, Finland, etc.) | Meeting target |
| <2% | Canada, Spain, Belgium, Italy, Portugal, Luxembourg | Below target |

### NATO's stance on LAWS (for the committee)

- NATO supports "**human control**" language but **opposes a blanket ban**.
- NATO states (US, UK, France, Germany, NL) push for **non-binding norms /
  the US Political Declaration** rather than a treaty.
- **This is the exact opposite of Iran's negotiating bloc** — Iran, Russia,
  China, Pakistan and the NAM/G-77 bloc favor a **legally binding instrument**.
