# Iran's UN Voting Record & Stances

How Iran votes and positions itself across the UN system — the voting bloc data
delegates need to predict and build coalitions on LAWS resolutions.

## Iran's UN General Assembly voting profile

| Characteristic | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Overall alignment | Anti-US, anti-Israel, pro-NAM/G-77, pro-Russia on most security votes |
| With-US alignment (UNGA) | Historically among the lowest (often <20%) |
| With-Russia alignment (UNGA) | Among the highest (often >80%) |
| With-China alignment (UNGA) | High (usually >80%) |
| Abstention strategy | Uses abstentions to preserve bilateral ties (Gulf, EU) |
| Voting record openness | Publishable via UN Digital Library |

## Iran's 5 stated LAWS principles (voting rationale)

1. **Sovereignty** — states have the right to develop defensive autonomous systems.
2. **Meaningful human control** — supported, but must not become a ban pretext.
3. **Equitable access** — no monopoly by major powers on military AI.
4. **Existing IHL applies** — CCW framework, not necessarily new binding law.
5. **Developing-country voice** — any treaty must reflect NAM/G-77 concerns.

## Coalition math for a LAWS resolution in DISEC

| Bloc | Approx. share | Iran's position |
|---|---|---|
| **NAM + G-77** | ~120+ states | For binding instrument — Iran's core bloc |
| **CCW "Group of 21"/NAM states** | ~80–100 | For binding instrument |
| **Russia, China, Pakistan** | — | For binding instrument (in GGE) |
| **EU + Western** | ~30 | For "human control" norms, against ban |
| **US-led (US, UK, allies)** | ~15–25 | Against binding instrument, for political declaration |
| **Gulf states** | ~6–10 | Split; Saudi/UAE close to US |

> **Iran's realistic coalition:** with NAM/G-77 + Russia + China, a "binding
> instrument" clause can command a **simple majority** but usually falls short of
> **consensus** — the negotiation is about forcing the US/EU to accept treaty
> language, not about passing a standalone resolution alone.

## Iran's abstention-prone votes (for coalition prediction)

- **Russia-Ukraine condemnations:** abstain (to protect Russia alliance).
- **TPNW-related provisions:** abstain (not a party, avoid hypocrisy).
- **Some Syria humanitarian votes:** abstain (defend Assad).
- **Iran human-rights resolutions:** vote NO / defend.

## How Iran frames LAWS in DISEC (from position paper)

- **Against:** "preemptive bans that freeze unequal capabilities."
- **For:** "multilateral, inclusive, legally binding regulation under the CCW."
- **Keyword set:** meaningful human control · accountability · transparency ·
  equitable access · sovereignty · dual-use · civilian protection.
