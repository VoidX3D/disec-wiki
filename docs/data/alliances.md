# Allied Nations & Strategic Partners

Iran's alignment map: formal allies, the "Axis of Resistance" network, strategic
partners, and hostile blocs. This is Iran's "friends and foes" dossier.

## The Axis of Resistance (Iran's core network)

The network of states and armed groups aligned with Iran, unified by opposition to
Israel, US presence in the region, and shared strategic interests.

| Partner | Type | Nature of alignment |
|---|---|---|
| **Hezbollah (Lebanon)** | Proxy / ally | Most capable proxy; extensive arms & training |
| **Syria (Assad govt)** | Ally | Kept in power via IRGC/Quds Force intervention (2011–) |
| **Iraqi Shia militias** | Proxy | PMF/Kata'ib Hezbollah, Asa'ib Ahl al-Haq |
| **Houthis / Ansarallah (Yemen)** | Proxy | Supplied missiles/drones; attacks Red Sea shipping |
| **Hamas & PIJ (Palestine)** | Proxy / ally | Funding, weapons, training |
| **Syrian government** | State ally | Deepest state-to-state alignment |
| **Lebanon (state)** | Weak/contested | Hezbollah dominates politics |

## Key non-allied but non-hostile states

- **Japan, South Korea** — major oil importers historically; now sanction-
  constrained.
- **Gulf states (Qatar, Oman, Kuwait)** — economic ties despite GCC tensions.
- **Turkmenistan, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan** — energy/economy ties.
- **Switzerland** — represents US interests in Iran (since 1980).

## The 25-Year China–Iran Deal (2021)

- **Duration:** 25 years; est. **US$400B** of investment in energy, infrastructure,
  banking and military cooperation.
- **Oil:** China is Iran's largest oil buyer (often "teapot" refineries).
- **Strategic:** Long-term hedge against US sanctions; deepens ties against the
  US-led order.

## Russia–Iran Strategic Partnership (2025)

- **Signed 17 January 2025** (Pezeshkian–Putin).
- **Military:** Su-35 fighters, S-400 air defense, satellite data, joint
  production.
- **Economic:** gas-for-goods, EAEU, North-South corridor.
- **Defense:** continued drone/missile cooperation; Iran arms shipments to Russia.

## Iran's permanent alliances — none

Iran is **not** in any formal mutual-defense treaty alliance (unlike NATO members
or the Five Eyes). Its security rests on:
1. **Asymmetric military capacity** (missiles, drones, proxies).
2. **Strategic partnerships** (Russia, China) that are transactional, not treaty-bound.
3. **Multilateral groupings** (BRICS, SCO, NAM) that give political cover.
