# Organizations & Institutions

International bodies, research institutes and civil-society coalitions shaping LAWS governance.

| Organization | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [United Nations Office for Disarmament Affairs (UNODA)](https://www.un.org/disarmament/) | Supports disarmament negotiations and arms control. Primary UN body for LAWS discussions. Facilitates GGE meetings. |
| [United Nations Institute for Disarmament Research (UNIDIR)](https://unidir.org/) | Conducts technical and policy research on emerging security technologies. Key source for LAWS analysis. |
| [International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC)](https://www.icrc.org/) | Humanitarian organization with mandate under Geneva Conventions. Most influential voice on IHL implications of LAWS. |
| [Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW)](https://www.un.org/disarmament/ccw/) | Primary legal framework for LAWS negotiations. 126 states parties. GGE established in 2016. |
| [Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (SIPRI)](https://www.sipri.org/) | Independent research institute tracking armaments, disarmament, and international security. Key data source. |
| [International Institute for Strategic Studies (IISS)](https://www.iiss.org/) | Independent research institute on defence and security. Military Balance is key reference. |
| [Campaign to Stop Killer Robots](https://www.stopkillerrobots.org/) | Coalition of 200+ NGOs advocating preemptive ban on fully autonomous weapons. |
| [Human Rights Watch (HRW)](https://www.hrw.org/) | Advocates for ban on fully autonomous weapons. Extensive research on humanitarian implications. |
| [Amnesty International](https://www.amnesty.org/) | Human rights perspective on LAWS. Emphasizes civilian protection and human dignity. |
| [Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)](https://www.oecd.org/) | Developed OECD AI Principles. First intergovernmental AI standards. |
| [NATO](https://www.nato.int/) | Developed AI Strategy emphasizing lawfulness, responsibility, explainability. 32 member states. |
| [African Union](https://au.int/) | Emphasizes responsible technology governance. Preventing technological inequality. |
| [European Union](https://europa.eu/) | Promoted trustworthy AI principles. AI Act sets global regulatory precedent. |
| [Arms Control Association](https://www.armscontrol.org/) | Independent membership organization tracking arms control developments. Key reference. |
