# Position Paper

> **Delegation:** Islamic Republic of Iran  
> **Committee:** DISEC (Disarmament and International Security Committee) Country: Islamic Republic of Iran Agenda: Regulating Lethal Autonomous Weapons Systems (LAWS) and Military Artificial Intelligence Artificial Intelligence (AI) has been rapidly transforming modern warfare through the development of Lethal Autonomous Weapons Systems (LAWS), which can identify and engage targets with limited or no human intervention. While these technologies may improve military efficiency and precision, they also raise significant legal, ethical, and humanitarian concerns. The possibility of machines making life-and-death decisions challenges the principles of International Humanitarian Law (IHL), particularly the principles of distinction, proportionality, precaution, and accountability. The rapid advancement of military AI has also increased concerns regarding an international AI arms race, the proliferation of autonomous weapons to non-state actors, and the absence of a universally accepted legal framework governing their development and use. Recognizing these risks, discussions continue under the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW) and its Group of Governmental Experts (GGE). International organizations such as UNODA, UNIDIR, and the ICRC have emphasized the importance of maintaining meaningful human control over the use of lethal force. As AI continues to reshape global security, establishing effective international regulations has become essential to ensure that technological advancement promotes international peace, security, and humanitarian principles.  
> **Agenda:** Regulating LAWS & Military AI

## Summary

The Islamic Republic of Iran recognizes both the opportunities and challenges presented by Artificial Intelligence in the military domain. Iran supports the peaceful development and application of AI while firmly maintaining that decisions involving the use of lethal force, including the potential deployment of autonomous military systems, must always remain under meaningful human control. The delegation believes that the regulation of Lethal Autonomous Weapons Systems (LAWS) should be based on the principles of the Charter of the United Nations, including the inherent right to self-defence under Article 51, International Humanitarian Law (IHL), and the framework of the Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW). Iran further emphasizes that any international framework must respect the sovereignty, security interests, and sovereign equality of all Member States, avoid discriminatory or politicized implementation, and ensure equitable access to AI technologies for the peaceful development of all nations, particularly developing countries. At the same time, Iran stresses that effective regulation must prevent the creation of technological monopolies or restrictive measures that deny developing nations access to legitimate defensive technologies. The Islamic Republic of Iran supports continued multilateral dialogue and negotiations under the United Nations and the CCW to establish practical, inclusive, and legally sound regulations that prevent an uncontrolled AI arms race while promoting transparency, accountability, international cooperation, and the responsible use of emerging technologies for the benefit of international peace and security.

The Islamic Republic of Iran has been actively participating in discussions about Lethal Autonomous Weapons Systems (LAWS) through the United Nations Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW) and its Group of Governmental Experts. Iran has consistently emphasized that International Humanitarian Law applies to all weapon systems and that human responsibility must remain central in all decisions involving the use of lethal force. Iran supports maintaining existing international legal frameworks while ensuring accountability, transparency, and compliance with humanitarian principles. Iran is a State Party to key international arms-control agreements, including the CCW, Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of Nuclear Weapons (NPT), Chemical Weapons Convention (CWC), and Biological Weapons Convention (BWC).Iran supports maintaining existing international legal frameworks while ensuring accountability, transparency, and compliance with humanitarian principles.Iran advocates for a balanced approach to military AI governance that prevents technological monopolization and ensures equal participation of developing countries. Iran will continue to support negotiations through the CCW framework as a practical and inclusive platform for addressing challenges related to autonomous weapons systems and their humanitarian impacts. The Islamic Republic of Iran calls upon the committee to establish a balanced and inclusive framework for addressing the challenges posed by Lethal Autonomous Weapons Systems. The regulation of military artificial intelligence must prioritize accountability, human responsibility, and compliance with International Humanitarian Law. The following aspects must be addressed accordingly: Ensuring meaningful human control over decisions involving the use of lethal force. Preventing the misuse, unauthorized transfer, and proliferation of autonomous military technologies. Guaranteeing that developing countries have equal participation in shaping international AI governance frameworks. Promoting transparency and confidence-building measures while respecting national sovereignty and security concerns. Ensuring that regulations do not become tools for technological monopolization by a limited number of advanced States. The Islamic Republic of Iran stands ready to support constructive international cooperation regarding LAWS. Iran believes that effective solutions must protect international security while respecting the sovereign rights and technological development of all nations. The following measures should be prioritized: Establish internationally accepted standards requiring meaningful human oversight in the development and deployment of autonomous weapons systems. Strengthen the role of the CCW Group of Governmental Experts as the primary platform for negotiating regulations on LAWS. Create international cooperation mechanisms to assist developing countries in understanding and safely managing military AI technologies. Promote transparency and reporting measures against and regarding the use autonomous weapons systems while protecting legitimate national security interests. Ensure that all future military regulations related to AI are developed through balanced multilateral engagement rather than selective limitations The Islamic Republic of Iran remains committed to addressing the challenges posed by Lethal Autonomous Weapons Systems through responsible international cooperation. Iran supports technological progress that contributes to security and development but emphasizes that autonomous weapons must remain governed by international law, human accountability, and the principles of equality and sovereignty among nations.
