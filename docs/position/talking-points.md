# Talking Points

## Opening Statement

Iran believes that all nations, regardless of technological capacity, must have an equal voice in shaping the governance of lethal autonomous weapons systems.

We recognize the legitimate security concerns of all states while emphasizing that the regulation of LAWS must not become a tool for preserving the technological dominance of a few powerful nations.

Iran has developed advanced drone capabilities not as an aggressor, but as a nation defending itself against decades of sanctions, threats, and security challenges in one of the world's most volatile regions.

We support meaningful human control over decisions involving the use of lethal force, but we reject any framework that would deny developing nations access to defensive technologies while major powers continue their own AI arms programmes.

The international community must address the accountability gap, ensure transparency, and prevent the proliferation of autonomous weapons to non-state actors, while respecting the sovereign right of states to develop legitimate defensive capabilities.

## Debate Points

- Any regulation must be non-discriminatory — it cannot restrict developing states while allowing major powers to continue their programmes.
- Existing international humanitarian law provides a foundation, but new instruments may be needed to address the unique challenges of autonomous systems.
- Meaningful human control must be defined inclusively, taking into account the diverse security environments of all states.
- The dual-use nature of AI technologies means that blanket restrictions could harm civilian technological development.
- Verification mechanisms must be practical, non-intrusive, and respect national sovereignty.
- Confidence-building measures and transparency can reduce the risk of accidental escalation involving autonomous systems.

## Closing Statement

Iran remains committed to multilateral dialogue and constructive engagement in addressing the challenges posed by lethal autonomous weapons systems.

We believe that a balanced, equitable, and inclusive approach to LAWS governance can serve the interests of all nations while protecting humanitarian principles.

Any international framework must recognize the legitimate security needs of all states, including the right to develop defensive capabilities essential for national survival.

Iran stands ready to work with all delegations to develop practical, implementable solutions that advance international peace and security.
