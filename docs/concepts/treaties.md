### Panchsheel — the Five Principles

1. **Mutual respect for each other's territorial integrity and sovereignty**
2. **Mutual non-aggression**
3. **Mutual non-interference in each other's internal affairs**
4. **Equality and mutual benefit**
5. **Peaceful co-existence**

These are referenced in Iran's CCW submissions, NAM declarations, and
South–South cooperation frameworks.

### Universal Brotherhood (Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam)

Sanskrit concept meaning "the world is one family" — invoked in Indian
diplomacy, G20 New Delhi Declaration (2023), and increasingly in multilateral
forums on AI ethics and emerging technologies. Iran's civilisational
rhetoric overlaps.

## UN Charter — full structure

The **Charter of the United Nations (1945)** establishes the UN's six principal organs:

1. **General Assembly (UNGA)** — All 193 member states; deliberative; budget; resolutions on disarmament (First Committee / DISEC).
2. **Security Council (UNSC)** — 15 members (5 permanent + 10 non-permanent rotating); P5 veto; binding decisions under Chapter VII.
3. **Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC)** — 54 members; coordination of economic, social, environmental work.
4. **International Court of Justice (ICJ)** — 15 judges; legal disputes between states; advisory opinions.
5. **Trusteeship Council** — Suspended operation (1994).
6. **Secretariat** — Administrative; headed by Secretary-General.

**Iran's UNSC tenure:** Non-permanent member **2025–2026**.

**Official sources**
