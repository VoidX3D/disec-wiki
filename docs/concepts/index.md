## Why these matter for DISEC

LAWS & Military AI governance sits at the intersection of:

1. **International Humanitarian Law (IHL)** — Geneva Conventions, Additional Protocols, CCW.
2. **Arms control & disarmament** — NPT, CWC, BWC, ATT, APMBC, CCM, TPNW.
3. **Human rights law** — UDHR, ICCPR, ICESCR, regional human-rights instruments.
4. **Diplomatic & treaty law** — Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties, Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations.
5. **Peaceful coexistence** — Panchsheel (Five Principles), Bandung Principles.
6. **Multilateral forums** — UN system, NAM, BRICS, SCO, regional organisations.

Delegates must navigate this dense legal and institutional landscape. The pages
that follow give every text, every category, every official source.

## Quick access by classification

### By binding force
- **Hard law (binding treaty):** NPT, CWC, BWC, CCW, Geneva Conventions, UDHR (declaratory), TPNW, ATT (where ratified)
- **Soft law (declarations, principles):** UDHR 1948, Panchsheel 1954, Bandung 1955, GGE outputs, UNGA resolutions
- **Customary law:** Hague Regulations 1907, many IHL principles

### By subject
- **WMD:** NPT, CWC, BWC, TPNW, CTBT, IAEA safeguards
- **Conventional:** CCW (incl. Protocols I–V), APMBC, CCM, ATT, UNROCA
- **IHL:** Geneva Conventions, Additional Protocols, Hague Regulations, Rome Statute
- **Human rights:** UDHR, ICCPR, ICESCR, CEDAW, CRC, CAT, regional charters
- **Diplomatic law:** Vienna Conventions 1961 & 1969
- **Environmental:** Stockholm, Basel, UNFCCC, Paris
- **Peaceful coexistence:** Panchsheel, Bandung Principles

### By parties (Iran-relevant)
- **Party:** NPT, CWC, BWC, CCW, Outer Space Treaty, ENMOD
- **Signed, not ratified:** CTBT, ATT
- **Non-party:** Geneva Conventions, Additional Protocols, APMBC, CCM, TPNW, Rome Statute
