## Bloc Quick Reference

A glance at how each delegation lines up on the central question — whether LAWS require a new legally binding instrument.

| Bloc | Countries | Position |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Responsible-use (anti-binding-treaty)** | United States, United Kingdom, France, Israel, Russia, China, Türkiye | Existing IHL suffices; prefer political declaration / principles over a treaty |
| **Strong binding-instrument** | Mexico, South Africa, Austria-style advocates, Switzerland, Netherlands (active), Germany | Support a legally binding instrument on LAWS |
| **Pragmatic middle (Global South)** | India, Pakistan, Brazil, Indonesia, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt, Nigeria, Kenya, Ethiopia, Bangladesh, Nepal, Vietnam, Argentina | Support regulation + human control but resist tech monopolies and restrictions on developing-state innovation |
| **Non-aligned / sovereignty-first** | Iran, DPRK | Reject frameworks seen as entrenching great-power advantage |
| **Conflict-experienced voice** | Ukraine | Emphasise real-world AI warfare lessons (drones, swarms, autonomy) |
