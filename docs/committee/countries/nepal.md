## LAWS & Military AI Stance

Nepal's position on lethal autonomous weapons systems is shaped by its constitutional commitment to peace, non-alignment, and multilateral diplomacy. As a state with limited military technological capacity, Nepal is unlikely to pursue the development of autonomous weapons or military AI programmes of its own.

Instead, Nepal's priorities focus on ensuring that emerging military technologies do not increase global inequality, destabilize smaller states, or undermine international peace. Kathmandu views disarmament not as a peripheral concern but as central to a just and equitable international order.

Nepal supports transparency, international regulation, and humanitarian approaches toward autonomous weapons governance. Its small-state perspective leads it to emphasize the importance of inclusive negotiations in which the voices of developing and smaller states are genuinely heard.

Nepal strongly supports meaningful human control over the use of lethal force, insisting that machines must never be permitted to make life-and-death decisions. Its position is aligned with humanitarian-minded states seeking stronger international standards.

As an NPT non-nuclear-weapon state and a CCW State Party, Nepal brings principled, non-aligned credibility to the committee, consistently advocating that disarmament and development advance together.

## Strategic Interests

- Preserving Nepal's constitutional principles of peace, non-violence, and non-alignment.
- Preventing technological inequality and domination of military AI by powerful states.
- Ensuring that small and developing states have a voice in global security governance.
- Supporting disarmament, the rule of law, and the UN disarmament machinery.
- Contributing to stability in South Asia, including through SAARC.
- Redirecting resources from militarism toward sustainable development.
- Upholding Nepal's long tradition of UN peacekeeping participation.

## Alliances & Memberships

- Non-Aligned Movement (NAM): principled non-alignment on security and disarmament issues.
- SAARC (South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation): regional cooperation and stability.
- Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW): State Party engaged in the GGE on LAWS.
- UN peacekeeping: long-standing troop-contributing country with a tradition of contributing to UN missions.

## UN Voting Record

Nepal has voted in favour of the consensus resolutions on lethal autonomous weapons systems in the First Committee, including A/RES/78/241 (2023) and A/RES/79/62 (2024). Kathmandu supports a legally binding instrument that emphasizes meaningful human control and accountability.

Nepal's humanitarian and non-aligned outlook aligns it with the bloc urging stronger restrictions, while it insists that any framework must be inclusive and safeguard the interests of small and developing states. Nepal consistently calls for universal participation and transparency in negotiations.
