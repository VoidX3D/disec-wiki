## LAWS & Military AI Stance

Vietnam's position on lethal autonomous weapons systems is influenced by regional tensions, particularly in the South China Sea. Hanoi seeks advanced defence capabilities but also supports international rules that prevent destabilizing military competition in its neighbourhood.

Vietnam supports responsible AI governance that respects national sovereignty. As a member of ASEAN, it prefers inclusive dialogue and confidence-building measures over confrontational arms control approaches, and it emphasizes the peaceful settlement of disputes under international law.

Vietnam leans toward the humanitarian end of the debate, supporting meaningful human control and full compliance with international humanitarian law. Hanoi has credible disarmament credentials as an NPT non-nuclear-weapon state and participates in the CCW GGE as a State Party.

At the same time, Vietnam is modernizing its defence forces, including growing experience with UAV and defence-industry development. It therefore seeks rules that prevent destabilizing competition and major-power dominance without denying states lawful means of self-defence and development.

Vietnam's approach reflects the broader ASEAN preference for stability, inclusivity, and the rule of law — a framework it sees as essential to managing the complex security dynamics of Southeast Asia and the South China Sea.

## Strategic Interests

- Maintaining stability and maritime sovereignty in the South China Sea.
- Promoting the peaceful resolution of disputes through international law.
- Upholding ASEAN centrality and the rules-based regional order.
- Modernizing defence capabilities without triggering a destabilizing arms race.
- Preventing major powers from dominating emerging military technologies.
- Ensuring access to peaceful AI technology for developing states.
- Sustaining disarmament credibility and the peaceful-use agenda of the NPT.

## Alliances & Memberships

- ASEAN: regional dialogue, confidence-building, and positions on South China Sea stability and the Code of Conduct.
- Non-Aligned Movement (NAM): Global South alignment on disarmament and technology access.
- Convention on Certain Conventional Weapons (CCW): State Party engaged in the GGE on LAWS.
- NPT framework: disarmament credentials as a non-nuclear-weapon state.
- Balanced strategic partnerships with major powers: diversify cooperation while preserving strategic autonomy.

## UN Voting Record

Vietnam votes in favour of the consensus resolutions on lethal autonomous weapons systems in the First Committee, including A/RES/78/241 (2023) and A/RES/79/62 (2024). Hanoi supports international rules and legally binding standards that enshrine meaningful human control and accountability.

While leaning humanitarian, Vietnam remains sovereignty-conscious. Through ASEAN it emphasizes dialogue, confidence-building, and the peaceful settlement of disputes, and it supports CCW GGE work while resisting provisions that could fuel destabilizing military competition in its region.
