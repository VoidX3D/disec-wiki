# PART X – TODAY’S NEED AND URGENCY TO ADDRESS THE AGENDA

The Immediate Requirement for Global Governance and Responsible Management of Lethal Autonomous Weapons Systems and Military Artificial Intelligence

The rapid advancement of Lethal Autonomous Weapons Systems (LAWS) and Military Artificial Intelligence has created an urgent requirement for international action. While artificial intelligence provides significant opportunities for improving defence capabilities, enhancing operational efficiency, and reducing risks faced by military personnel, the absence of comprehensive governance mechanisms creates serious risks for international peace, humanitarian protection, and strategic stability.

The urgency of addressing this issue arises from the widening gap between technological development and international regulation. Artificial intelligence technologies are advancing at an unprecedented pace, while diplomatic negotiations and legal frameworks are progressing comparatively slowly. If international institutions fail to establish effective norms before autonomous capabilities become widespread, future regulation may become significantly more difficult.

The challenge is not to prevent technological advancement entirely but to ensure that military applications of artificial intelligence remain consistent with international law, human responsibility, and humanitarian principles. The international community must establish mechanisms that allow beneficial innovation while preventing uncontrolled autonomy in decisions involving the use of lethal force.

1. Preventing an Uncontrolled Military Artificial Intelligence Arms Race

One of the strongest reasons for immediate international action is the possibility of an uncontrolled military AI arms race. As major powers increasingly recognize artificial intelligence as a strategic advantage, competition between states may encourage rapid development without sufficient consideration of humanitarian consequences.

The competition between technologically advanced countries demonstrates how strategic rivalry can accelerate military innovation. States may fear that restricting their own development will create disadvantages compared with competitors who continue expanding autonomous capabilities.

Unlike traditional weapons races, an AI arms race presents unique challenges because artificial intelligence technologies evolve rapidly and are closely connected with civilian industries. Software, algorithms, robotics, and computing systems developed for civilian purposes can often be adapted for military applications.

This dual-use nature makes traditional arms control approaches less effective. Without international cooperation, states may prioritize speed and strategic advantage over safety, transparency, and ethical considerations.

Establishing international norms at an early stage is therefore essential to prevent military AI competition from becoming destabilizing.

2. Protecting Civilian Populations from Uncontrolled Autonomous Warfare

The protection of civilians represents one of the strongest humanitarian reasons for addressing LAWS urgently. Modern conflicts increasingly occur in populated areas where military operations take place among civilian communities.

Autonomous weapons operating in complex environments may face difficulties distinguishing between combatants and civilians. Even advanced artificial intelligence systems may struggle with unpredictable human behaviour, changing battlefield conditions, and incomplete information.

Civilian protection requires that military technologies remain subject to meaningful human judgment. Human decision-makers are capable of considering context, uncertainty, and moral consequences in ways that current AI systems cannot fully replicate.

The absence of clear international standards may increase the possibility that autonomous systems are deployed before their reliability and humanitarian consequences are adequately understood.

Therefore, establishing safeguards before widespread deployment is essential for preventing unnecessary civilian suffering.

3. Maintaining Meaningful Human Control Over Decisions of Life and Death

The preservation of human control is considered one of the most important principles in discussions regarding autonomous weapons. Decisions involving the use of lethal force carry ethical, legal, and moral responsibilities that should remain connected to human judgment.

The concept of meaningful human control does not necessarily require humans to manually operate every function of a weapon system. Instead, it emphasizes that humans must maintain sufficient involvement in understanding, supervising, and controlling military operations.

Without meaningful human control, there is a danger that responsibility becomes unclear and decisions involving human life become reduced to algorithmic processes.

## International action is therefore required to establish common standards regarding

• The level of human involvement required before deployment

• The responsibilities of commanders and operators

• The limits of autonomous decision-making

• The procedures for reviewing and controlling autonomous systems

Without such standards, different countries may adopt significantly different approaches, increasing uncertainty and strategic risk.

4. Establishing Clear Accountability Mechanisms

A major urgency in regulating Military AI is addressing the accountability gap. Existing legal systems are based on the assumption that humans make decisions and can therefore be held responsible.

Autonomous systems challenge this assumption because responsibility may be distributed among multiple actors, including military commanders, software developers, technology companies, and manufacturers.

Clear accountability mechanisms are necessary to ensure that autonomous systems do not create situations where unlawful actions occur without responsible individuals being identified.

## International frameworks should therefore establish principles regarding

• Legal responsibility throughout the weapon development process

• Documentation and operational records

• Human oversight requirements

• Investigation mechanisms after incidents

Maintaining accountability is essential for preserving international humanitarian law and preventing the misuse of autonomous technologies.

5. Preventing Proliferation to Irresponsible Actors

The accessibility of artificial intelligence technologies creates significant proliferation concerns. Unlike nuclear weapons, which require highly specialized materials and infrastructure, many autonomous technologies rely on commercially available components.

Drones, robotics, sensors, and AI software are increasingly accessible worldwide. This creates risks that autonomous capabilities could spread to states with weak regulatory systems or to non-state actors.

Terrorist organizations, criminal groups, and insurgent movements may attempt to modify commercially available technologies for harmful purposes.

Therefore, international cooperation is necessary to develop mechanisms addressing:

• Responsible technology transfers

• Export controls

• Monitoring of military AI development

• Prevention of misuse by non-state actors

Failure to address proliferation risks may make future conflicts more unpredictable and dangerous.

6. Strengthening International Legal and Ethical Frameworks

Existing international humanitarian law provides important principles for regulating warfare, but emerging technologies require further clarification.

The international community must determine how principles such as distinction, proportionality, and precaution apply to autonomous systems.

## Additional international frameworks should clarify

• Which forms of autonomy are acceptable

• Which applications require restrictions

• What level of human involvement is necessary

• How compliance should be verified

Ethical frameworks are equally important because not every technological possibility is necessarily morally acceptable.

The development of military AI requires balancing strategic interests with universal principles of human dignity and responsibility.

7. Ensuring Equality Between Technologically Advanced and Developing States

Another urgent issue is preventing technological inequality from increasing global security disparities.

Military AI development is currently concentrated among technologically advanced states with strong research institutions, advanced computing infrastructure, and defence industries.

Developing countries may become dependent on foreign suppliers for military technologies or excluded from important decisions regarding global security governance.

International efforts should therefore ensure that discussions regarding Military AI include diverse perspectives from all regions.

Developing countries should have meaningful participation in establishing global rules rather than becoming passive recipients of decisions made by technologically dominant powers.

8. Improving Transparency and Confidence-Building Between States

The lack of transparency regarding military AI development increases mistrust among states. Countries may assume that competitors are developing more advanced autonomous capabilities than publicly acknowledged.

## Confidence-building measures can reduce uncertainty by encouraging states to share information regarding

• Military AI principles

• Testing procedures

• Safety mechanisms

• Human oversight policies

Such transparency measures do not require states to reveal sensitive military information but can help prevent misunderstanding and unnecessary escalation.

9. Preparing International Institutions for Future Technological Change

The rapid evolution of artificial intelligence demonstrates that future military technologies may emerge faster than traditional diplomatic processes can respond.

International institutions must therefore develop flexible approaches capable of adapting to technological change.

Rather than creating regulations only after harmful consequences occur, international governance should adopt preventive approaches based on risk assessment and responsible innovation.

The regulation of Military AI can become a model for governing future emerging technologies that influence global security.

10. The Need for Immediate Multilateral Cooperation

The complexity of Military AI means that no single country can effectively address the challenge alone. Autonomous weapons development is influenced by global technology markets, international security competition, and cross-border innovation.

Therefore, cooperation among governments, international organizations, scientific communities, technology companies, and humanitarian organizations is essential.

## A successful international approach should combine

• Legal regulation

• Ethical principles

• Technical standards

• Transparency mechanisms

• Responsible innovation

• Humanitarian safeguards

The objective should not be preventing technological progress but ensuring that technology remains under human control.
