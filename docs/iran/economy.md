## Sanctions exposure

- **US:** Primary + secondary sanctions, OFAC SDN list includes IRGC,
  Khatam al-Anbiya, Bank Mellat, Bank Saderat, Post Bank, hundreds of
  entities.
- **EU:** Restrictive measures (asset freezes, oil embargo).
- **UN:** Measures lifted under JCPOA (2016); "snapback" provisions lapsed
  October 2025 — see [Treaties](treaties.md).
- **SWIFT:** Cut for Iranian banks since 2018.
- **Settlement workarounds:** barter (oil-for-goods), crypto (USDT-heavy grey
  market), China CIPS, Russia SPFS, INSTEX (Europe — largely dormant).
