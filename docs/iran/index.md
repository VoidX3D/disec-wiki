## Summary

Iran supports international negotiations on LAWS but emphasizes the right of
states to develop defensive capabilities. Iran advocates for a balanced approach
that considers both humanitarian concerns and national security needs. Tehran
supports meaningful human control while opposing frameworks that could restrict
access to advanced defence technologies.

## Core stance

Iran opposes the militarization of AI by major powers while defending its
sovereign right to develop asymmetric military capabilities, including
autonomous systems, as a deterrent against regional and extra-regional threats.

## Key principles

- Sovereign equality of all states in shaping global governance
- Right to self-defence under Article 51 of the UN Charter
- Opposition to technological monopolization by major powers
- Support for multilateral negotiations under UN frameworks
- Emphasis on inclusive governance reflecting interests of all states
- Recognition that existing IHL is applicable but may need adaptation
- Opposition to unilateral coercive measures affecting technology access
