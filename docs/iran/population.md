## Age structure

Iran has a **maturing youth bulge**. The fertility transition accelerated after
the 1980s family-planning programme; the share of working-age adults is large.

| Age group | Share | Population |
|---|---|---|
| 0–14 | 22.6% | ~20.9 M |
| 15–24 | 15.7% | ~14.5 M |
| 25–54 | 45.4% | ~42.0 M |
| 55–64 | 9.0% | ~8.3 M |
| 65+ | 7.3% | ~6.8 M |

> Dependency ratio: ~42 dependents per 100 working-age adults. Below many
> regional peers; a demographic dividend window that policy debates often cite.

## Diaspora

- **Iranian diaspora worldwide:** estimated **4–6 million** people.
- Major concentrations: USA (~1.0–1.5 M, esp. Los Angeles "Tehrangeles"),
  Canada (~250K, esp. Toronto), Germany (~300K), UK (~80K), UAE, Turkey,
  Sweden, Israel (historical), Australia.
- Iran is a major **source of highly-educated migrants** — the "brain drain"
  is a recurring policy debate; remittances are estimated at $0.5–1B annually.
