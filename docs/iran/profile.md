## Voting Pattern

Iran has consistently voted in favour of UNGA resolutions on disarmament and arms control. Iran voted for the CCW framework and supports multilateral approaches to LAWS governance. Iran has NOT supported proposals perceived as favouring technologically dominant states at the expense of developing countries.
