# Organizations & Memberships

Every organization Iran belongs to (or engages with) and what Iran does there
— the institutional map of Iran's foreign policy and its LAWS strategy.

![Flag of Iran](/img/iran.png)

## United Nations system

| Body | Iran status | Role for Iran |
|---|---|---|
| **UN General Assembly** | Full member (since 1945) | Active in disarmament (DISEC). Voting bloc leader on LAWS. |
| **UN Security Council** | Non-permanent 2025–2026 | Sits during LAWS negotiations. |
| **IAEA** | Member (1958) | Nuclear safeguards — central dossier. |
| **OPCW** | Member (1997) | CWC implementation. |
| **WHO** | Member | Health cooperation. |
| **UNESCO** | Member | Heritage, education, AI ethics. |
| **UNODC** | Member | Drug-trafficking control (Afghan border). |
| **UNDP** | Member | Development programmes. |
| **UN Human Rights Council** | Non-member since 2020 (held 2018–20) | Criticised record. |
| **UNHCR** | Member | Afghan refugee hosting (~3.4 M). |

## Disarmament & arms-control venues

| Forum | Iran's role |
|---|---|
| **CCW GGE on LAWS** | **Active participant**; supports binding instrument. |
| **UNGA First Committee (DISEC)** | **Voting bloc leader** on LAWS resolutions. |
| **NPT Review Conferences** | Non-nuclear-weapon-state bloc; criticises NWS non-compliance. |
| **Conference on Disarmament (CD)** | Member; backs Fissile Material Cut-off negotiation. |
| **UN Register of Conventional Arms** | Reports partially. |
| **UNIDIR** | Think-tank; engages on AI / autonomy. |
| **ICRC** | Humanitarian-law counterpart; dialogue partner on LAWS. |

## Economic & political blocs

| Organization | Since | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Non-Aligned Movement (NAM)** | 1979 (founding spirit; chair 2012–13) | Ideological core. |
| **Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC)** | 1969 | Largest Muslim bloc. |
| **BRICS** | 2024 (invited 2023) | New full member. |
| **Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)** | 2023 full member | China/Russia-led security bloc. |
| **Economic Cooperation Organisation (ECO)** | 1985 | Regional trade. |
| **OPEC** | 1960 | **Founding member.** |
| **Gas Exporting Countries Forum (GECF)** | 2001 | Gas exporters. |
| **Group of 77 (G-77)** | 1967 | Developing-country bloc in UN. |
| **EAEU Free Trade Agreement** | 2023 | Russia-led economic bloc. |
| **ASEAN** | Dialogue partner | Limited engagement. |
| **Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB)** | Member | Infrastructure financing. |
| **New Development Bank (NDB / BRICS Bank)** | Member (via BRICS) | Alternative to World Bank. |

## Organisations Iran is NOT in

| Organization | Status | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **NATO** | Non-member (hostile) | NATO framed as US-led threat to Iran. |
| **CSTO** | Observer only | Iran closer to Russia but not bound. |
| **Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC)** | Non-member (rivalry with Saudi/UAE) | Gulf rival bloc. |
| **Five Eyes** | Non-member | Western intelligence alliance. |
| **Quad / AUKUS** | Non-member | Indo-Pacific anti-China posture. |
| **Missile Technology Control Regime (MTCR)** | Non-member | Self-restraint by choice. |
| **Wassenaar Arrangement** | Non-member | Dual-use export-control regime. |
| **Financial Action Task Force (FATF)** | Non-member | Iran on blacklist; sanctions impact. |
| **Paris Club** | Non-member | Sovereign-debt restructuring. |
| **World Trade Organization (WTO)** | Observer since 2005 | Observer only, not member. |
