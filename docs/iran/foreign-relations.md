Iran's strategic partners, regional rivals and the "Axis of Resistance" network.

## Strategic partners & supporters

### Russia

![Russia](/img/flags/ru.svg)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Strategic depth | "Strategic partnership" elevated 2025 |
| Trade | ~US$5 B/yr (targeted $10 B) |
| Defence ties | Drone co-production, S-300 air defence, Su-35 talks, Mi-28 |
| Syria | Joint backer of Assad government 2015–2024 |

### China

![China](/img/flags/cn.svg)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|

## The "Axis of Resistance"

Iran's regional network of non-state allies and proxy forces.

| Group | Country | Iran's support | LAWS relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hezbollah** | Lebanon | ~US$700 M/yr est. | Demonstrated drone use; tactical missile expertise. |
| **Houthi movement** | Yemen | Drones, missiles, training | Active use of cruise / ballistic missiles and loitering munitions. |
| **Kata'ib Hezbollah** | Iraq | Arms, training | Drone attacks on US bases. |
| **Asa'ib Ahl al-Haq** | Iraq | Arms, training | Drone and rocket capability. |
| **Popular Mobilisation Forces (PMF)** | Iraq | Political backing, some arms | Various factions; Shia coalition. |
| **Hamas** | Palestine | Limited technical aid (pre-2023) | Paraglider / drone use Oct 2023. |
| **Palestinian Islamic Jihad** | Palestine | Historical ties | Rocket / drone capability. |
| **Syrian militias** | Syria | IRGC-coordinated | Cross-border operations. |
