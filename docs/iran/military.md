## Structure

Iran fields **two parallel forces**:

- **Artesh** (regular armed forces) — Army, Navy, Air Force under the General
  Staff. Legacy of the pre-revolution Imperial Iranian Armed Forces.
- **IRGC / Sepah** — Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, founded 1979. Runs its
  own land, naval (IRGCN), air (IRGC ASF), aerospace and cyber commands plus the
  **Basij** militia. Controls the strategic missile programme.

## Navy & IRGCN

- **Submarines:** 3 Kilo-class (Russia) + ~30 midget / coastal (Ghadir, Nahang,
  Fateh-class). One of the largest midget-submarine fleets in the world.
- **Surface combatants:** frigates (Jamaran, Sahand, Alvand-class), corvettes,
  IRGCN fast-attack craft squadrons with anti-ship missiles (hundreds of small
  boats — the asymmetric "swarm" model).
- **Strategy:** "asymmetric naval warfare" focused on the Strait of Hormuz and
  Persian Gulf. Capable of harassing but not defeating a major blue-water navy.

## Cyber & electronic warfare

- Multiple **Advanced Persistent Threat (APT)** groups attributed to the IRGC
  (e.g. APT33 / Refined Kitten, APT35 / Charming Kitten, OilRig).
- Domestic **electronic warfare** suite integrated with air defence.
- **Offensive cyber** programmes targeting Israeli, Saudi and US infrastructure.

## Autonomous & AI-assisted capabilities

- **Swarm tactics** demonstrated in regional operations (Strait of Hormuz, Red Sea
  theatre).
- **Autonomous navigation** + GPS-denied waypoint navigation on Shahed / Mohajer.
- **AI-assisted target identification** claimed on later-generation UCAVs.
- **Precision-guided munitions** across ballistic and cruise missile families.
- **Electronic warfare integration** with anti-jam datalinks (SATCOM + RF).
- **Low-cost high-volume** production model — the central economic argument in
  the LAWS debate (a $20K Shahed can force a $2M interceptor).

## Nuclear status

Iran is a **non-nuclear-weapon state party** to the NPT (ratified 1970), with
IAEA Comprehensive Safeguards Agreement in force. See [Treaties](treaties.md)
and the [Nuclear & Mines](../data/nuclear-and-mines.md) dossier for the full
JCPOA / snapback / enrichment timeline.

## Strengths & limitations

### Strengths
- Largest drone fleet in the Middle East, battle-tested
- Massive ballistic-missile inventory, indigenous production
- Mature cyber warfare capability
- Asymmetric doctrine designed to impose cost, not win attritional war
- Large domestic defence industry (DIO, AIO, IRGC Khatam al-Anbiya HQ)
- Combat experience from proxy operations (Syria, Iraq, Yemen, Lebanon)

### Limitations
- Sanctions limit access to advanced semiconductors and avionics
- Fighter fleet largely pre-1979 vintage (F-14, F-4) — air-superiority gap vs.
  modern NATO/Israeli 5th-generation fleets
- Naval forces are coastal / asymmetric, not blue-water
- Limited space-based ISR
- Heavy reliance on reverse-engineering of foreign subsystems
- Ageing armour and conventional platforms despite advanced unmanned systems
