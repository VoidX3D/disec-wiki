# Military Capabilities

> Iran has developed significant asymmetric military capabilities despite economic sanctions and technological limitations. Iran is a major drone power with extensive experience in autonomous systems.

## Strengths

- Advanced drone programme (Shahed series, Mohajer series,Ababil series)
- Indigenous missile technology with precision strike capability
- Cyber warfare capabilities (Advanced Persistent Threat groups)
- Asymmetric warfare doctrine emphasizing deterrence
- Large domestic defence industry (DIO, AIO)
- Experience with proxy warfare and unmanned systems

## Limitations

- Economic sanctions limit access to advanced semiconductors and components
- Air force largely relies on pre-revolution aircraft (F-14, F-4)
- Naval capabilities focused on coastal defence and asymmetric warfare
- Limited space-based surveillance assets
- Dependence on reverse-engineering foreign technology
- Ageing conventional platforms despite advanced unmanned systems

## Autonomous Capabilities

- Swarm tactics demonstrated in regional operations
- Autonomous navigation and target identification
- Real-time ISR (intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance)
- Precision strike with guided munitions
- Electronic warfare integration
- Low-cost high-volume production model
