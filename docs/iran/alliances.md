# Alliances & Alignments

## Supportive States

### Russia

Strong. Both oppose US-led tech restrictions. Coordinate in CCW. Similar positions on sovereignty and non-interference.

### China

Moderate. Both oppose Western-dominated governance frameworks. China supports inclusive LAWS discussions. Economic ties through 25-year cooperation agreement.

### Pakistan

Moderate. Shared concerns about Indian military AI. Both support developing country participation in governance.

### India

Moderate. Both support balanced approach. India and Iran share concerns about technological inequality.

## Neutral States

### Brazil

Both support inclusive governance and developing country participation. Differ on some regional issues.

### Mexico

Both support meaningful human control. Mexico more oriented toward complete ban.

### South Africa

Both support Global South interests and equitable governance.

## Opposing States

### United States

Fundamental strategic rivalry. US opposes Iran's military programmes. Different visions for LAWS governance.

### Israel

Deep strategic rivalry. Israel possesses advanced autonomous systems. Opposes Iran's military capabilities.

### Saudi Arabia

Regional competition. Gulf security dynamics. Saudi opposes Iran's regional influence.

### France

Supports EU regulatory approach. May support restrictions Iran views as discriminatory.

### United Kingdom

Close to US position. Supports responsible use framework Iran views as insufficient.
