#  South Korea records its highest-ever temperature of 42.5 degrees Celsius 

- **Source:** Japan Times
- **Published:** Sun Aug 02 2026
- **Original:** <https://www.japantimes.co.jp/news/2026/08/02/asia-pacific/south-korea-record-temperature/>

The southeastern city of Yangsan saw the temperature reach 42.5 C at 1:26 pm, the Korea Meteorological Administration said on its website.
