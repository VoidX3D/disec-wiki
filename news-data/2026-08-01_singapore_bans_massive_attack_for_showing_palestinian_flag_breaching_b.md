# Singapore bans Massive Attack for showing Palestinian flag, breaching ban on foreign emblems

- **Source:** The Guardian
- **Published:** Sat Aug 01 2026
- **Original:** <https://www.theguardian.com/music/2026/aug/01/singapore-bans-massive-attack-for-showing-palestinian-flag-breaching-ban-on-foreign-emblems>

Two members given ‘stern warnings’ after concert and barred from returning, with band also excluded from performing in Singapore again, say authorities British trip-hop band Massive Attack has been barred from performing in Singapore after an investigation found two members unfurled a Palestinian flag during a concert, police said.
