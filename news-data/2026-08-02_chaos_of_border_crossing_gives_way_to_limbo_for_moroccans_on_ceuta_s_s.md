# Chaos of border crossing gives way to limbo for Moroccans on Ceuta’s streets

- **Source:** The Guardian
- **Published:** Sun Aug 02 2026
- **Original:** <https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/02/ceuta-border-crossing-spain-morocco>

People among the more than 50,000 who entered Spanish enclave this week describe the journey and their prospects Four days ago, Youssef El Abbas saw an opportunity to reclaim the life he had lost seven years earlier. News was circulating that about 400 people had swum from Morocco to the neighbouring Spanish enclave of Ceuta on the north African coast, approximately 12 miles (19km) across the strait of Gibraltar from mainland Spain. Here was a chance for the 44-year-old Moroccan father of four to return to Spain, from which he had been deported when his residency expired after the loss of his job in Bilbao in 2019.
